# ResetMinute App Store Metadata Draft

Date: April 4, 2026

## Purpose

This is the first submission-ready English metadata draft for the ResetMinute MVP.

It is intentionally narrow:

- iPhone-first
- general wellness
- desk-worker habit loop
- no medical-device or treatment framing

## Product framing rules

Every field below should stay consistent with the approved product boundary:

- desk resets
- neck, shoulder, and back discomfort during long workdays
- optional posture awareness
- short movement breaks
- local-first processing

Do not introduce:

- diagnosis language
- treatment, therapy, rehabilitation, or prevention claims
- guaranteed pain relief claims
- clinical validation claims
- real-time monitoring promises that do not exist

## Core App Store fields

### App name

`ResetMinute`

### Subtitle

`Desk Breaks for Neck & Back`

### Primary category

`Health & Fitness`

### Secondary category

`Productivity`

Reason:

- primary value is a wellness reset habit
- secondary value is workday interruption and routine support

## Promotional text

`Take a quick desk reset for your neck, shoulders, and back during long workdays. Build a healthier desk routine with optional reminders and gentle weekly progress tracking.`

## Short positioning line

Use this as the internal anchor for any other metadata variants:

`ResetMinute helps desk workers interrupt long sitting sessions with short guided resets and optional posture check-ins.`

## Description draft

### Opening paragraph

`ResetMinute helps desk workers take short breaks for neck, shoulder, and back discomfort during long workdays. Start a quick reset in seconds, keep optional check-ins secondary, and build a healthier desk routine one workday at a time.`

### Body

`Built for desk-heavy workdays`

- `Start a short desk reset without a complicated setup`
- `Use optional weekday reminders to step away and move`
- `Keep posture check-ins optional instead of making the camera your daily workflow`
- `See simple weekly progress for completed resets and active days`
- `Keep your saved check-ins and journal data on your device`

`ResetMinute is designed for general wellness and desk habits. It is not a diagnostic or treatment app.`

### Closing paragraph

`If you spend long hours sitting, ResetMinute helps you make short movement breaks easier to start and easier to repeat.`

## Keyword draft

Recommended first keyword set:

`desk break,stretch break,neck reset,back reset,shoulder reset,work break,sitting break,posture,desk stretch,wellness`

Keyword rules:

- keep the first set English-only
- avoid `pain relief`, `therapy`, `treatment`, `rehab`, `medical`
- prefer search terms tied to habit and desk use

## Screenshot messaging direction

Final screenshot assets are handled separately, but the first metadata set should assume this story:

1. `Start a desk reset in seconds`
2. `Optional reminders for long workdays`
3. `Neck, shoulder, and back reset flows`
4. `Camera check-ins stay optional`
5. `Track your week, not a clinical score`

## Review notes draft

Use review notes that reduce App Review confusion:

`ResetMinute is a general-wellness iPhone app for desk workers. The main flow is short desk reset routines plus optional local reminders. Camera-based posture check-ins are optional, secondary, and processed on device. The app is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition.`

If the first submission still includes local save of check-in photos or pose data, add:

`Check-in photos and pose-related artifacts are stored locally on device only when the user chooses to save a journal entry.`

## Privacy-answer alignment reminders

Before submission, confirm App Store Connect answers match the real app:

- camera is used only for optional check-ins
- reminders use local notifications
- check-in images are not silently uploaded
- pose-related data is not silently uploaded
- saved journal data stays local unless the product changes later

## Localization plan for the first submission

Recommended first submission scope:

- one English metadata set first
- do not localize until the English product language and screenshots are stable
- if localization starts later, every locale must keep the same no-certification boundary

## Fields to hold back for MVP

Do not overreach in the first submission:

- no claim-heavy app preview script
- no subscription-heavy promotional copy
- no fake premium capabilities
- no `AI posture alerts` language
- no `clinic` or `program` framing
