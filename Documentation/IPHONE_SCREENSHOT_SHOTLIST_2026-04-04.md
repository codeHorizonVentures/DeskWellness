# ResetMinute iPhone Screenshot Shot List

Date: April 4, 2026

## Purpose

This is the first iPhone screenshot plan for ResetMinute MVP.

It is designed to support the current product direction:

- quick desk resets first
- reminders as a workday habit tool
- optional posture check-ins
- weekly progress without clinical framing

## Capture rules

- use `ResetMinute` branding, not `DeskWellness`
- keep copy inside the general-wellness boundary
- do not show clinical scores or diagnosis language
- do not show empty, confusing, or prototype-looking states

## Capture-ready launch arguments

These arguments are available now for repeatable screenshot setup:

- `-ResetMinuteForceOnboarding`
- `-ResetMinuteSkipOnboarding`
- `-ResetMinuteDemoConsistency`
- `-ResetMinuteOpenReminderSettings`

Recommended combinations:

1. Onboarding
- `-ResetMinuteForceOnboarding`

2. Home with weekly progress
- `-ResetMinuteSkipOnboarding`
- `-ResetMinuteDemoConsistency`

3. Reminder settings
- `-ResetMinuteSkipOnboarding`
- `-ResetMinuteDemoConsistency`
- `-ResetMinuteOpenReminderSettings`
