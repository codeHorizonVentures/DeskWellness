# DeskWellness Naming Decision

Date: April 3, 2026

## Decision

Use:

`ResetMinute`

Recommended App Store subtitle:

`Desk Breaks for Neck & Back`

## Scope of this decision

- `DeskWellness` remains the repo and internal project/program name for now
- `ResetMinute` is the recommended public product name
- this is a naming and positioning decision, not a legal trademark clearance

## Why ResetMinute wins

### 1. It matches the real product

The rethought MVP is about:

- short desk resets
- quick repeat use
- neck, shoulder, and lower-back discomfort management during long desk sessions

`ResetMinute` fits that much better than `DeskWellness`, which sounds broad and generic.

### 2. It is clearer than the other finalists

Compared with other strong candidates:

- `BreakKind` was too abstract and had obvious search noise from software terminology
- `DeskMend` sounded more therapeutic than needed
- `SitShift` was catchy but had industrial search noise and was less calm
- `WorkReset` had existing web/domain overlap
- `QuietReset` had existing overlap

`ResetMinute` is the best balance of clarity, tone, and distinctiveness.

### 3. It supports the right brand promise

The name naturally suggests:

- short duration
- light friction
- everyday repeatability
- a non-clinical wellness experience

## Naming guidance

Use the brand like this:

- app name: `ResetMinute`
- subtitle: `Desk Breaks for Neck & Back`

Good supporting phrases:

- short resets for desk discomfort
- gentle breaks for long workdays
- quick neck, shoulder, and back resets
- build a healthier desk routine

Avoid turning the brand back into:

- a posture clinic
- a medical or pain-treatment app
- a broad generic wellness utility

## Quick overlap summary

As of April 3, 2026:

- `DeskReset`, `DeskRest`, `StretchDesk`, `DeskFlow`, and `DeskEase` already have meaningful market overlap
- `QuietReset` and `WorkReset` also show existing overlap/noise
- `ResetMinute` did not show the same level of obvious product-market collision in the quick App Store and web pass

This is still not trademark clearance. It is a practical product-naming recommendation based on current public overlap checks.

## Next implication

If we keep this decision, the next product-facing assets should use `ResetMinute`:

- Jira product brief
- App Store planning docs
- marketing copy drafts
- onboarding and in-app copy drafts
