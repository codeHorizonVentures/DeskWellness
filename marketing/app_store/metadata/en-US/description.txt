ResetMinute is a desk break reminder for office and remote workers who sit for long hours. Start a guided stretch reset in seconds, keep posture check-ins optional, and build a healthier workday routine.

Built for desk-heavy workdays

- Start a short desk break or stretch reset without a complicated setup
- Use optional weekday reminders to step away, move, and reset during work
- Keep posture check-ins optional instead of making the camera your daily workflow
- See simple weekly progress for completed resets and active days
- Keep your saved check-ins and journal data on your device

ResetMinute is designed for general wellness and desk habits. It is not a diagnostic or treatment app.

If you spend long hours sitting, ResetMinute helps make desk breaks, stretch reminders, and short movement resets easier to start and easier to repeat.
